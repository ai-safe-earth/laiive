# Pro onboarding (pro settings phase 5, items 9-10)

Fields to collect when someone becomes pro (minimal, progressive):
- What you are: venue, artist/group, promoter (sets the org kind).
- Organization display name (public). Your role in it (free text, context only).
- Contact email (prefilled from Google, private). Phone (optional, private).
- Website or social profile (optional; the best evidence for claim review).

Never collect tax or legal identity until laiive handles money (GDPR liability).
Keep the public profile and private contact details separate from the start.
